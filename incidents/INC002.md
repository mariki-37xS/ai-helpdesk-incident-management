# INC002 - Employee Account Lockout

## Incident Details
- Incident ID: INC002
- Category: Identity and Access Management
- Subcategory: Account Lockout
- Impact: Low
- Urgency: High
- Priority: P3 - Moderate
- Status: Resolved
- Assignment Group: IT Service Desk

## Short Description

Employee unable to access their company account.

## Description

An employee reports being locked out of their
corporate account after multiple unsuccessful
login attempts. The employee cannot access
company email or internal applications.

## Business Impact

The employee is unable to perform normal
work duties until account access is restored.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Verify the employee's identity using approved procedures.
2. Review the reported authentication error.
3. Check the account lockout status.
4. Review failed login attempts.
5. Check for suspicious account activity.
6. Follow the approved account recovery procedure.
7. Verify successful login and MFA authentication.

## AI-Assisted Analysis

Potential causes include repeated incorrect
password attempts, expired credentials,
cached credentials, or suspicious login activity.

## Security Considerations

- Never request the employee's password.
- Verify identity before unlocking the account.
- Never bypass MFA or security policies.
- Escalate suspicious activity to the security team.

## Resolution

- Verified the employee's identity.
- Reviewed authentication logs.
- Confirmed authorization for account recovery.
- Updated outdated saved credentials securely.
- Unlocked the account using approved procedures.
- Verified successful login and MFA authentication.
- Confirmed access to company applications.

## Root Cause

Outdated cached credentials following a recent
password change were the likely cause of
repeated authentication failures.

## User Confirmation

The employee confirmed that account access,
email, and internal applications were restored.
