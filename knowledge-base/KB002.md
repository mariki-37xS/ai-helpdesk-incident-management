# KB002 - Secure Employee Account Recovery

## Article Information
- Article ID: KB002
- Category: Identity and Access Management
- Subcategory: Account Recovery
- Version: 1.0
- Status: Draft
- Audience: IT Support Analysts

## Purpose

This article provides guidance for securely
restoring access to locked employee accounts.

## Common Symptoms

- Employee unable to sign in.
- Account locked after failed login attempts.
- Unable to access company email.
- Authentication errors in internal applications.

## Possible Causes

- Repeated incorrect password attempts.
- Expired passwords.
- Outdated cached credentials.
- Suspicious authentication activity.

## Troubleshooting Instructions

1. Verify the employee's identity using approved procedures.
2. Confirm the account lockout status.
3. Review authentication logs.
4. Identify the source of failed login attempts.
5. Check for outdated saved credentials.
6. Escalate any suspicious activity before proceeding.
7. Perform authorized account recovery.
8. Guide the employee through updating saved credentials.
9. Verify successful login and MFA authentication.
10. Confirm access to authorized company applications.

## Security Considerations

- Never request or disclose employee passwords.
- Never request MFA verification codes.
- Never bypass identity verification.
- Follow company security policies.
- Protect confidential employee information.

## Expected Result

The employee securely regains access to
authorized company applications.

## Escalation Procedure

Escalate unresolved authentication issues
to the Identity and Access Management Team.

Report suspected account compromise to
the Information Security Team.
